The TCUP Hub Data Science special interest group (SIG) is a home for Tribal
College and Native Hawaiian-serving institution faculty who want to keep
building data science and AI skills after the workshop, sharing reusable
examples and review practices while keeping people and communities in control.

This SIG is supported by the National Science Foundation through the TCUP Hub,
the Environmental Data Science Innovation and Impact Lab (**ESIIL**), and Oglala
Lakota College's **Thokatakiya** project (**NSF Award # 2225573**).

# Who it's for

Faculty and staff at Tribal Colleges and Universities and Native
Hawaiian-serving institutions who came to a TCUP Hub data science workshop, or
who want to join later.

# Data governance

- Members are listed by their own consent, and can ask to be removed at any
  time.
- Shared examples use no community data without that community's approval.
- We follow the [FAIR Principles](https://www.go-fair.org/FAIR-principles/)
  and the
  [CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance](https://www.gida-global.org/careprinciples),
  and point to OCAP for community data ownership.
- For a model of this in practice, see the
  [Ethics](https://tyson-swetnam.github.io/intro-gpt/#ethics) and
  [About This Workshop](https://tyson-swetnam.github.io/intro-gpt/#about-this-workshop)
  sections of Tyson Swetnam's Intro to GPT site.
- Our [AI use statement](AI_USE.md) says how AI tools were used to make this
  site and what we ask of contributors.
